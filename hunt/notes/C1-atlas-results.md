## Finding 1 — the quantum mixer's gap never closes

Minimum Davies gap over all 72 points: **1.0003** (quasiperiodic, β = 2,
h = 8). Random fields *increase* the gap (up to 1.88 at β = 0.25). At this
system size, every knob setting we probed leaves the quantum sampler
rapidly mixing — the "keep the mixer" half of the Bakshi–Tan recipe looks
robust to inhomogeneity.

## Finding 2 — the knob ordering: disorder protects entanglement

Moderate fields first *enhance* Gibbs entanglement (peak at h ≈ 1–2;
log-negativity up to **0.95** at β = 2) — then the profiles diverge sharply
at strong field:

| log-negativity at h = 8 | β = 0.5 | β = 1 | β = 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| uniform | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| quasiperiodic | 0.021 | 0.026 | 0.050 |
| **random** | **0.196** | **0.310** | **0.448** |

Uniform strong fields re-productize the state (all spins align — classically
trivial again). **Disorder frustrates the re-productization**: random fields
keep the state far from separable at field strengths where the uniform
profile has made it classical. Ordering: random > quasiperiodic > uniform.

## Finding 3 — Bakshi–Tan's regime is invisible at n = 5

At β = 0.25 (inside their β < 1 hardness regime, crossover h* ≈ 5.5),
negativity is ≈ 0 everywhere, with only whispers (~10⁻³) at h = 8. Their
entanglement-induction mechanism is asymptotic; small systems don't show it.
The region where *our* numerics show maximal structure — **β ∈ [1, 2],
h ∈ [1, 2]: entanglement near maximal, gap open** — is intermediate
temperature: exactly the seam where neither the easiness atlas nor the
existing hardness results reach. That is C1's target region, now with
coordinates.

## What this changes in the C1 brief

1. Knob priority *reversed*: random fields (was knob 3's cousin) now lead;
   quasiperiodic second; both beat uniform at preserving structure.
2. Target regime: intermediate β with moderate-to-strong disordered fields —
   not the β < 1 corner.
3. Next numerics, in order: disorder-average (10 seeds) at n = 5; push to
   n = 6–7 (superoperator 4096²–16384², feasible on the 96-core node);
   implement the cluster-expansion convergence certificate to overlay
   "where the classical certificate dies" on these maps.
4. Then the proof decision: one family, chosen where (gap open) ∧
   (certificate broken) ∧ (a hardness-inheritance route exists).
