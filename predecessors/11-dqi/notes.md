2026 state (what got killed, what stands) lives there. Primary source:

## 1. The problem

**max-LinSAT:** given constraints over a finite field — for us the two key
cases — satisfy as many as possible:
- max-XORSAT: linear equations aᵢ·x = bᵢ over F₂;
- **OPI (Optimal Polynomial Intersection):** find a degree < k polynomial
  Q over F_p maximizing the number of points i where Q(yᵢ) lands in a
  prescribed set Fᵢ. Equivalently: noisy polynomial reconstruction — a
  problem with a real cryptographic pedigree (Reed–Solomon list decoding,
  Naor–Pinkas oblivious polynomial evaluation).

## 2. The classical wall

For OPI: the natural attacks are algebraic (lattice/Coppersmith-style
noisy-interpolation — per the sweep, known variants don't reach it) and
information-set decoding (Prange), which achieves satisfied fraction
~ 1/2 + o(1)-ish in the relevant regime. For *random sparse* instances the
wall turned out to be on the QUANTUM side — see §4.

## 3. The primitive

The genuinely new idea of the decade, and it is problem-first to the bone:

1. The objective f(x) = Σᵢ ±(indicator of constraint i satisfied) has
   Fourier support concentrated on **low-weight combinations of the
   constraint vectors** — i.e., on words of the code generated by the aᵢ.
2. DQI prepares the state Σₓ P(f(x))|x⟩ (amplitudes = degree-ℓ polynomial
   of the objective!) by working on the Fourier side: superpose weight-≤ℓ
   "error patterns" y, then **uncompute y from its syndrome — which is
   exactly syndrome decoding** of the dual code.
3. Measuring gives samples concentrated on high-objective x. The satisfied
   fraction follows the paper's **semicircle law**: roughly, decoding
   radius ℓ buys objective quality; a better decoder = a better optimizer.

The architecture, named for our catalog: **optimization → coding theory →
borrow the decoder.** Where Shor turned factoring into Fourier sampling,
DQI turns optimization into decoding. A 60-year-old classical toolbox
(algebraic codes and their decoders) becomes a quantum-algorithmic resource.

## 4. The hardness evidence — the 2026 ledger (from the sweep; verify)

- **Killed:** random/sparse max-k-XORSAT — three independent classical
  lines (OGP/AMP obstructions; tight inapproximability; enhanced Prange
  matching even genuinely quantum LDPC decoders — the DQI team's own
  negative result, Apr 2026). No advantage without algebraic structure.
- **Standing:** OPI over F_p with Reed–Solomon structure — explicitly
  searched for classical attacks in the sweep: none found, best classical
  still Prange-type. Also standing, younger: Hermitian/AG-code OPI,
  rank-metric (Gabidulin) variants, and HDQI (Gibbs sampling via decoding
  — the bridge to ground C).
- Sun–Wootters: DQI's semicircle law is not optimal on worst-case OPI —
  headroom exists (for either side).
